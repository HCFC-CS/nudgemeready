# What still needs doing — useful, in life, easy, natural

The master product prompt was not a request to rebuild NMR, add a 10-tab bar, or turn wellbeing into a calorie app.

It was this: **help someone get through real days without shame** — remember what matters, get a nudge at the right moment, start even when it feels hard, and feel a small win when something is sorted.

This note is the work that still stands between “engines exist” and “this is part of my life.”

## What “useful / in life / easy / natural” means

A person should be able to:

1. **Say or tap what they need** in a few seconds — “bins tomorrow 7pm”, “I need to pay the council tax”, “have a drink”.
2. **Get a gentle nudge** when it is time — on the lock screen, optionally spoken, optionally when they leave the house. Not only if they open the app.
3. **See what’s coming** without hunting — today, later this week, later on — one timeline.
4. **Do something about it from the list** — Sorted, Later, make it smaller, skip today — without filling a long form first.
5. **Keep phone life in the same place** — appointments that match the calendar, people from Contacts, lists, money dates.
6. **Ask for help** when they want it, without the app becoming a second social network.
7. **Feel a treat coming**, not a streak they can fail.

Phases 1–4 on this branch already added the *supportive behaviour* (rewards glance, why-hard, stalled items, find-it, optional drink/meal/move lists). They did **not** yet make the daily path short or the reminders reliable. That is the remaining usefulness work.

## What already works (do not rebuild)

- Six “what do you want to do?” paths on Add, with no Ready4 pack required.
- One list of nudges, one Reward Bank, one Coming Up timeline, one My Money.
- Sorted / Later / Ask / Remove, Focus, make it smaller, why is this hard.
- Optional shop ideas that never force a purchase.
- Ready4 packs as extra templates, not separate apps.

If those feel hidden, that is a **path** problem, not a missing engine.

## What needs to be done (in order)

Do these in this order. Each one should make the app feel more like a companion. Stop when the daily path feels natural — do not keep adding features from the prompt that do not help a real morning.

### 1. First useful minute

**Problem today:** After signup you often land on an empty Nudges list. Add always opens a long Item Details screen. Push reminders are off until Settings.

**Do:**

- Empty Home and Nudges invite one first action: *Add something you don’t want to forget.*
- From Add, **save a dated reminder without the long form** when the person already said what and when (voice or a short “remind me…” line).
- On first save, ask once, calmly, to turn on notifications — not buried in Settings.

**Done when:** a new person can go from open app → “remind me to take the bins out tomorrow evening” → it exists on Nudges and Coming Up, in under about three taps or one voice save.

### 2. Nudges that reach you in life

**Problem today:** Speaking reminders only fire for some item types, and only if push is on. The morning “Your day ahead” ping does not name what is actually waiting. Leaving-home reminders need extra place setup.

**Do:**

- Schedule a lock-screen nudge for anything with a time — reminders, appointments, routines — not only the “reminder” type.
- Daily summary lists today’s real titles (or stays silent if nothing is due). Quiet hours still respected.
- Leaving-home / pay-later only after a simple “this is home” or “this is the shop” save — no lab of settings first.

**Done when:** if something is due at 9:00, the phone says so at 9:00 even if the app is closed.

### 3. Act from the list (easy and natural)

**Problem today:** Confirm-done and swipe-delete are on the list. Sorted, Later, make it smaller, and why-hard live on Item Details and Focus. Most people will never find them.

**Do:**

- On each Nudges row, offer **Later** and **Sorted** without opening the item.
- Keep **Make it smaller** and **Why is this hard?** one tap away (sheet or Focus), not only after scrolling a long details screen.

**Done when:** a hard morning can be handled from the list: later / smaller / skip — no form, no guilt.

### 4. One life timeline as the habit

**Problem today:** Coming Up is good, but the app opens on a working list. Untitled drafts often have no date, so the timeline stays empty.

**Do:**

- Home should lead with *what’s coming up*, then one next action (Focus or Add).
- Capture should default a sensible time when the person didn’t pick one (e.g. later today), and make changing it obvious.
- Phone calendar pull should be a single clear choice: *Show my appointments here* — then they appear on Coming Up.

**Done when:** the person can answer “what’s my day?” from Home without opening Menu.

### 5. Voice that actually files the thought

**Problem today:** Say it still drops you on Item Details. Dates sometimes parse; the save is not finished.

**Do:**

- “Remind me to call mum Thursday 4pm” → saved reminder with that time, optional confirm screen, then done.
- “I ate something” / “short walk” already map — keep them as lists, not trackers.

**Done when:** speaking a thought is as trustworthy as typing it.

### 6. People and help, honestly

**Problem today:** Crew on this phone is real (roles, invites, Ask drafts). Live shared care across two phones is not. The master prompt’s “integrated into life” includes other people, but cloud sync was never built.

**Do:**

- Make **Ask** obvious on the item (and from the list), opening a ready message.
- Keep Crew hub as Invite — no extra pressure CTAs.
- Be clear in-app: *Crew on this device* until multi-device exists. Do not fake a live shared list.

**Done when:** asking for help is two taps, and nobody thinks the other person magically sees the nudge on their own phone.

### 7. Only after the above feels natural

These were in the master prompt. They polish motivation and shopping. They do **not** make the app useful if reminders still don’t fire.

- Reward pictures / a dream list
- “That was hard” extra points after finishing
- Richer Help me find it (local shops, registries)

**Still do not do** (prompt had them; they fight the product):

- Calorie counting, barcode, BMI, weight-loss points
- A 10-tab bar
- A second points wallet, second task list, or second calendar
- Renaming Sorted to Done
- Inflating points to 5 / 10 / 20

## Suggested build order

| Next | Job | Why first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | First useful minute + notification ask | Without this, nothing else is in life |
| B | Timed nudges + a real morning summary | This *is* integration with the day |
| C | Later / Sorted / smaller from the list | This *is* easy and natural |
| D | Home leads with Coming Up; capture gets a time | The timeline becomes the habit |
| E | Voice save that actually saves | Natural capture |
| F | Ask-for-help from the list; honest Crew copy | Shared care without pretending |

A–C change how the app feels every day. D–F make it belong in life. Pictures and shop extras wait.

## How this relates to Phases 1–4

Phases 1–4 made the *tone and extras* match the prompt (rewards you can see, adapt when stuck, find-it without pressure, optional wellbeing lists).

**A–F make the core loop short enough to use.** That is the remaining master-prompt work that matters.
