Privacy Policy — Nudge me Ready

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Nudge me Ready keeps your nudges, notes, appointments, crew details, and attachments on your device. This version does not upload your nudge data to our servers for cloud sync or backup.

Depending on features you use, the app may request microphone, notifications, location, contacts, calendars, camera/photos, and Face ID permissions. You can refuse or revoke them in phone Settings.

Calendar and contact features use accounts already on your phone (such as iCloud, Google, or Outlook). Crew invite links should only be shared with people you trust.

Contact: support@nudgemeready.app

Full policy text is also available in the app under Settings → Privacy & support.
