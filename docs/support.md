## Getting started

1. Open the app, create your profile, then optionally set up Face ID, a PIN, or a password.
2. Add a nudge from **+nudge**, or browse Notes, Reminders, Appointments, and Lists.
3. For appointments, add guests from Contacts and optionally link to your phone calendar.
4. Read the full illustrated [User manual](https://nudgemeready.app/manual/) for every screen and setting.

## Common questions

### Why can’t I use Face ID / voice / calendar yet?
Those features need a full app install from TestFlight (or a development build), not Expo Go. Install the latest TestFlight build after each native update.

      Where is my data stored?
On your phone. This version does not sync nudges to the cloud. For everyday privacy: keep a passcode on the phone, turn on app lock in Settings, and store your recovery code offline (paper or a password manager — not only in Notes on the same phone). That covers a lost phone and casual snooping well for this kind of app. Forgetting your PIN or password only resets the lock — it does not delete your nudges. Uninstalling the app removes local data.

### How do Crew invites work?
Share the invite link from **Invite Crew**. The other person opens it on their phone with Nudge me Ready installed. The link carries the invite details so they can accept even if they never had the invite before.

### I forgot my PIN or password
On the sign-in screen, choose **Forgot PIN?** / **Forgot password?** and confirm with Face ID / device passcode, or enter your recovery code.

### Notifications don’t speak or open the nudge
Allow notifications for Nudge me Ready in iPhone Settings. Reminder nudges need a date/time and speaking text where relevant.
