Workflow

  1. Tap Add.
  2. Say it — for example, remind me to take the bins out tomorrow evening.
  3. If you said what and when, it saves straight away.
  4. Or pick Plan it, Remember it, Do it, Book & go, Buy & pay, or Life & people.
  5. Or tap Something else and type in your own words.
  6. The first save may ask to turn on quiet phone reminders.

Good to know

  • The six paths work with no Ready4 pack.
  • Wedding, moving, baby and other pack choices stay hidden until that pack is installed.
