1. Welcome

Nudge me Ready helps you remember what matters, get a gentle nudge at the right moment, and take a small next step when something feels hard.

This guide matches v3 0.3.1 (the TestFlight app named Nudge me Ready v3). It sits next to the older 0.2.0 app if you still have that installed. The two apps do not share data.

Screenshots are from this version. On your phone, names and times will be yours — not the sample items shown here.

2. Getting started

  1. Install Nudge me Ready v3 from TestFlight and open it.
  2. Choose a photo or emoji, then enter your name, email, and date of birth.
  3. Accept Terms of Use, then tap create / continue.
  4. Optionally set a password or PIN (and Face ID). You can skip this and turn it on later in Settings.
  5. If a recovery code is shown, save it offline, then continue.
  6. On the welcome screen, tap See my day to open Home.

4. Add something

Tap the round Add button. You do not need a Ready4 pack for everyday reminders.

Say it (fastest)

  1. Tap Say it and speak naturally — for example, “remind me to take the bins out tomorrow evening”.
  2. If you said what and when, it saves straight away and appears on Nudges and What’s coming up.
  3. The first save may ask, calmly, to turn on quiet phone reminders. You can say not now.

Voice needs the TestFlight app (not a web preview) and microphone permission.

5. Nudges — Later, Sorted, Smaller, Ask

Nudges is your working list. You do not have to open an item to take a kind action.

ChipWhat it does
LaterMoves it to tomorrow. That is allowed.
SortedMarks it done and can add a small Reward Bank point.
SmallerOffers a tinier first step when it feels like too much.
AskOpens Ask for help about that item (a message from this phone).

Filter with Open / All / Done, or Dates / To-dos / Lists. Search if the list is long. Completing, snoozing, or skipping never costs you anything in the app.

6. Home and What’s coming up

Home leads with What’s coming up — today, then later — so you see the next real thing without hunting.

  • Tap See what’s coming up for Today / 7 days / Month / later.
  • Focus on this starts a calm session on the next item.
  • Show my appointments here (when offered) pulls phone calendar events onto the same timeline. Holidays and similar noise are skipped.
  • If the timeline is empty, Home invites you to Add something you don’t want to forget.

7. Focus

  1. Open Focus.
  2. If something feels heavy, use Why is this hard today? or take the smaller step offered.
  3. Start the timer. Pause, skip, or stop whenever you need — there is no failed streak.

8. Reward Bank

One bank for the whole app. Points stay small on purpose: a quiet treat, not a score you can fail.

Sorted and I did something can add points. Claim when it feels kind — Favourite coffee, a £10 treat, and other options you can rename.

9. Ready4Packs

Everyday paths work with no pack. Ready4 packs add extra templates into those same paths — they are not separate apps.

  • Open a pack to preview, then install. Items appear in Nudges and you can edit every one.
  • Pack-specific choices (wedding, moving, baby, and so on) only show after that pack is installed.
  • On TestFlight, if purchases are off, the app says you will not be charged and still lets you try packs.
  • Medication, emergencies, and similar organisation are not medical advice. Follow your clinician. In immediate danger, contact emergency services.

10. Crew and Ask

Crew is people you trust. In this version, Crew lives on this phone. Invites and Ask open a message you send yourself — the other person does not see your list live on their phone yet.

  1. Menu → Crew → Invite.
  2. Choose Email, SMS, WhatsApp, or Copy link.
  3. From a Nudges row, Ask can mention that item in the message.
Nudge me Ready is everyday support, not an emergency service. If someone is at immediate risk, contact emergency services.

12. My money

Menu → My money works with no Ready4 pack: what’s coming in, going out, and left — no judgement. Ready4 project budgets only appear if you install that pack.

13. Privacy

  • Nudges, notes, attachments, and settings stay on this phone and are encrypted at rest.
  • This version does not sync to the cloud. A reinstall starts empty.
  • Permissions (notifications, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, Face ID, photos) are only used for features you turn on.
  • Full policy: Privacy policy

14. Tips

Reminders don’t fire

Use the TestFlight v3 app, not a web preview. iPhone Settings → Nudge me Ready v3 → Notifications, then in-app Push. Quiet hours hold alerts overnight on purpose.

I still have the old app

Keep using Nudge me Ready 0.2.0 if you were testing v2. Open Nudge me Ready v3 for this guide. Both icons can sit on the Home Screen.

Need a hand

Email support@nudgemeready.app with your iPhone model, iOS version, what you were trying to do, and a screenshot if it helps.
